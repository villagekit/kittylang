# notes

The `note` collection, declared in [.kipu/collections/note.toml](../.kipu/collections/note.toml):
knowledge that is neither a plan nor a decision. A pinned item, of any
collection, is what `kipu context` injects; notes are where most live.

The first two carry what the old `NOTES.md` held: the reading list, and
the open questions and todos that are not yet plans.
